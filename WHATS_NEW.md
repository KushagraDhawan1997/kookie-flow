# What's new in Kookie Flow

## October 15

1. **Swappable skins.** The glass look is its own package, `@kushagradhawan/kookie-flow-skin`. The canvas reads colors, sizes and materials from the skin.
2. **Old package removed.** The all-in-one `@kushagradhawan/kookie-flow` package is gone. Use the core, WebGL and React packages.
3. **New docs address.** The docs live at flow.kookie.design, with the K mark and a work-in-progress notice.
4. **Kookie Flow vs React Flow.** A new study compares the two for Womp.

## October 14

1. **No motion.** Canvas controls appear at once, to match Kookie UI.

## September 25

1. **Three packages.** Kookie Flow splits into core, WebGL and React packages.

## September 19

1. **Studio agent.** Studio has an agent panel in every graph, and Home asks the agent.

## September 17

1. **Studio models and templates.** Studio adds a models catalogue, model pages and curated templates. Graphs move to /graphs.

## September 15

1. **Studio app.** Studio is a node app built on Kookie Flow, with accounts, a prepaid balance through Stripe, and generation jobs that keep running when the tab closes.

## September 11

1. **Media on the canvas.** Pictures, video and 3D models sit on the canvas with glass controls. Paste a picture and it appears.
2. **Drawing.** A pen draws ink on the canvas.
3. **Editing tools.** Undo and redo, alignment guides, align and distribute, and keyboard moves for nodes.
4. **Node previews and progress.** A node can show what it produced, and its outline shows progress.

## September 10

1. **New look.** Nodes are floating squircle cards with a typographic header, edges glow, and the grid is dots. The bundled font is Inter.

## September 9

1. **Runs on Kookie UI.** Kookie Flow moves to Kookie UI, and widgets and labels draw in WebGL.
