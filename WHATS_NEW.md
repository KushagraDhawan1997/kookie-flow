# What's new in Kookie Flow

Each day groups its changes as Major, Minor or Quiet. Major is a new product or a change to what it is. Minor is a change you notice when you use it. Quiet is a small touch, or how the work is made.

## October 21

### Minor

- **Front-page hero.** The docs front page opens with a live graph: a Generate node wired into the picture it made.

### Quiet

- **Work-in-progress notice at the top.** The notice sits above every page instead of on two, and stays dismissed after a reload.

## October 16

### Minor

- **Zoomed-out boards.** When you zoom out, edges thin to fine lines and cards keep a clear edge.

### Quiet

- **Skin package renamed.** The skin package is now `@kushagradhawan/kookie-flow-skin`, and its export is `skin`.

## October 15

### Minor

- **Swappable skins.** The glass look is its own skin package. The canvas reads colors, sizes and materials from the skin.
- **New docs address.** The docs live at flow.kookie.design, with the K mark and a work-in-progress notice.

### Quiet

- **Old package removed.** The all-in-one `@kushagradhawan/kookie-flow` package is gone. Use the core, WebGL and React packages.
- **Kookie Flow vs React Flow.** A new study compares the two for Womp.

## October 14

### Quiet

- **No motion.** Canvas controls appear at once, to match Kookie UI.

## September 25

### Quiet

- **Three packages.** Kookie Flow splits into core, WebGL and React packages.

## September 19

### Minor

- **Studio agent.** Studio has an agent panel in every graph, and Home asks the agent.

## September 17

### Minor

- **Studio models and templates.** Studio adds a models catalogue, model pages and curated templates. Graphs move to /graphs.

## September 15

### Major

- **Studio app.** Studio is a node app built on Kookie Flow, with accounts, a prepaid balance through Stripe, and generation jobs that keep running when the tab closes.

## September 11

### Minor

- **Media on the canvas.** Pictures, video and 3D models sit on the canvas with glass controls. Paste a picture and it appears.
- **Drawing.** A pen draws ink on the canvas.
- **Editing tools.** Undo and redo, alignment guides, align and distribute, and keyboard moves for nodes.
- **Node previews and progress.** A node can show what it produced, and its outline shows progress.

## September 10

### Minor

- **New look.** Nodes are floating squircle cards with a typographic header, edges glow, and the grid is dots. The bundled font is Inter.

## September 9

### Minor

- **Runs on Kookie UI.** Kookie Flow moves to Kookie UI, and widgets and labels draw in WebGL.
